## 🛠️ Featured Business Intelligence & Data Case Studies

### 1. 🏦 Bank Loan Risk Segmentation & Default Modeling
- **Tech Stack**: `SQL` · `MySQL Workbench`
- **Dataset Scope**: 3-table relational database (*Customers*, *Loans*, *Payments*) spanning 50 customers and payment histories across Home, Personal, and Car loan verticals.
- **Analytical Deliverable**: Executed 12+ analytical queries identifying loan default risk factors. Correlated credit scores (<650) and income brackets with default probability to optimize credit line allocation strategies.

### 2. 📉 Customer Churn Drivers Analysis
- **Tech Stack**: `Python` · `Pandas` · `NumPy` · `Matplotlib` · `Seaborn`
- **Dataset Scope**: 7,043 subscription records across 21 behavioral and demographic metrics.
- **Analytical Deliverable**: Conducted comprehensive EDA establishing a ~26.5% baseline attrition rate. Identified customer tenure under 12 months as the single primary predictor of churn, formulating targeted retention recommendations.

### 3. 💼 Freelancer Marketplace Transaction Behavior
- **Tech Stack**: `SQL` · `MySQL Workbench` · `Advanced Excel`
- **Dataset Scope**: 6-table relational architecture (*Users*, *Profiles*, *Projects*, *Bids*, *Contracts*, *Reviews*) containing 200+ transactional records.
- **Analytical Deliverable**: Authored 14 complex analytical SQL queries evaluating bidding dynamics. Established that top-rated freelancers capture >65% of enterprise contracts.

### 4. 🎮 Behavioral Gaming & Mental Health Analytics
- **Tech Stack**: `Power BI` · `DAX`
- **Dataset Scope**: Multi-dimensional survey dataset tracking gaming intensity and psychological well-being metrics.
- **Analytical Deliverable**: Constructed an interactive Power BI dashboard featuring dynamic DAX measures and drill-through slicers to isolate at-risk behavioral profiles.

### 5. 👟 Global Footwear Retail Sales Executive Dashboard
- **Tech Stack**: `Power BI` · `Excel Pivot Models`
- **Dataset Scope**: Global sports footwear retail sales across multiple geographic regions, product categories, and temporal periods.
- **Analytical Deliverable**: Developed a multi-page executive reporting suite tracking revenue trends, product category profitability, and regional unit distribution.
